Company Brief — Round 1: The Discounted Delivery

The Discounted Delivery: E-Commerce Crisis Blueprint

Client: RapidKart Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Industry: E-Commerce & Logistics
Founded: 2016 | Headquarters: Bengaluru, India

📦 Company Overview

RapidKart is one of India’s fastest-growing e-commerce platforms, built on the bold promise of “Free and Fast Delivery — Everywhere, Every Time.” Over the past few years, RapidKart has captured massive market share through aggressive discounting, a vast logistics network, and a hyper-competitive shipping policy that guarantees 10-minute urban delivery for key SKUs.

The company operates two major divisions:

  • RapidKart Direct (1P Model): Products sourced and sold directly by RapidKart.
  • RapidKart Marketplace (3P Model): Third-party sellers listing on the platform and paying commissions on each sale.

While the platform’s popularity skyrocketed post-2022, internal reports now hint at a growing imbalance between speed, cost, and control.

📉 The Current Situation

Despite record order volumes, RapidKart’s financial health has sharply deteriorated:

  • Net profit margins have dropped by nearly 80% year-on-year.
  • Warehousing and delivery costs have risen disproportionately compared to revenue growth.
  • Seller retention is falling, with mid-tier merchants exiting the platform citing “non-competitive visibility.”
  • Internal departments appear to be operating in silos, each under immense pressure to sustain RapidKart’s market image.

Employees have also reported growing concerns around unrealistic operational targets, morale decline among warehouse teams, and tensions between the finance, marketing, and logistics divisions.

To the public, RapidKart remains a success story — but internally, it’s clear that something deeper is eroding the business model’s foundations.

🧩 Your Mandate

You are part of a consulting task force commissioned to perform an emergency strategic audit of RapidKart.

Your mission is to:

  • Identify the root causes behind RapidKart’s profitability decline.
  • Analyze how internal decisions across departments might be interlinked.
  • Present a comprehensive set of findings and insights highlighting what’s going wrong beneath the surface.

At this stage, your goal is not to propose solutions — only to investigate, question, and uncover.

⚙️ Competition Context

  • In this round, participants will interact with AI-based RapidKart department chatbots (Finance, Operations, Marketing, HR, etc.).
  • Each chatbot represents an internal employee and will respond based on its department’s version of the truth.
  • Your objective is to connect the dots across departments and discover what’s being hidden in plain sight.

🧭 End Goal of Round 1

  • Articulate the underlying structural and ethical issues affecting RapidKart.
  • Gather key information to support your Executive Summary Report (Round 2).
