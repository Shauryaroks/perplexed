RAPIDKART — FINAL PITCH DECK ROUND

Round 3: Strategic Recovery & Repositioning

Objective:
Develop and present a comprehensive strategic solution that addresses RapidKart’s multi-layered crisis — financial, operational, and ethical — while ensuring long-term sustainability and marketplace trust.

⚙️ Deliverable Format

  • Type: Pitch Deck Presentation (PPT)
  • Slides Limit: Maximum 4 content slides (excluding cover, thank you, or annexure).
  • Font & Visuals: Clear, minimal, and readable; visuals and frameworks encouraged.
  • Duration (if presented live): 7–10 minutes

🎯 Expected Focus Areas

Participants are free to structure the deck as they wish, but the solutions should holistically address:

Financial Integrity & Profitability:

  • Making the business model financially sustainable and transparent.
  • Correcting reporting mechanisms, cost allocation, and pricing frameworks.

Operational Stability & Control:

  • Strengthening warehouse and delivery efficiency without compromising audit integrity.
  • Building accountability and reducing process-level risks.

Ethical & Marketplace Rebuilding:

  • Restoring parity and trust between first-party (1P) and third-party (3P) sellers.
  • Ensuring long-term stakeholder and reputational recovery.

Strategic Cohesion:

  • Connecting the above three levers under a unifying vision for RapidKart’s turnaround.
  • Defining a clear, measurable roadmap with timelines or impact metrics.
